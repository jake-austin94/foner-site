FAQs

Questions? Answers.

Is the month really free?

Yes. No card details, no contract and nothing to cancel. If foner hasn’t brought you work by the end of the month, I switch it off and that’s it.

What happens after the free month?

We look at the calls it caught together. If it’s paying for itself, we agree a simple monthly price before anything is charged. If not, you walk away.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Your phone rings as normal. If you don’t answer, the call forwards to your foner number, so customers keep dialling the number they already know. Turning it off is one short code.

Will callers know it’s AI?

It introduces itself as your assistant, and if anyone asks whether it’s AI, it tells them.

Does it give prices or book jobs?

It never quotes prices. As standard, it takes the caller’s details and what they need, so you can get back to them. If you’d like it to book appointments straight into your calendar, like the lash and brow example above, I can set that up for you.

Who is it for?

UK trades and small service businesses: roofers, plumbers, electricians, cleaners, salons and anyone who can’t always get to the phone.

FAQs

Questions? Answers.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. Your phone rings as normal. If you don’t answer, the call forwards to your foner number, so customers keep dialling the number they already know.

Will callers know it’s AI?

It introduces itself as your assistant, and if anyone asks whether it’s AI, it tells them.

Does it give prices or book jobs?

No. It never quotes prices or confirms bookings you haven’t agreed. It takes the caller’s details and what they need, so you can get back to them.

How do I get the details?

You get a summary by email straight after every call: name, number, address, the job and anything else they mentioned.

Who is it for?

UK trades and small service businesses: roofers, plumbers, electricians, cleaners and anyone who can’t always get to the phone.
